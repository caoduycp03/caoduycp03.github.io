A general framework for text- and image-conditioned 3D generation post-training across single- and multi-stage models.

01 / Abstract

Recent large-scale pretrained 3D generators are powerful, yet their outputs are not explicitly optimized for user preferences or downstream tasks. They also face conditioning-specific limitations: text-conditioned models capture broad semantics but often lack fine-grained visual quality, while image-conditioned models preserve visible appearance but may produce implausible unseen regions.

We introduce Next3DGen, a unified reward-based post-training framework for text- and image-conditioned 3D generation across single- and multi-stage architectures. It uses conditioning-specific reward pipelines to improve visual quality, condition alignment, and 3D plausibility. For image-to-3D generation, complementary rewards preserve alignment with the reference image while improving unseen regions. For multi-stage generators, tree-structured rollouts jointly optimize geometry and texture through stage-specific credit assignment. Next3DGen consistently improves diverse pretrained generators while remaining training-efficient.
