Duy Cao (aka Cao Duy)

Qualcomm Research Resident · 3D Generation and Understanding · Robotics

I am an AI researcher interested in how machines can generate, understand, and interact with 3D worlds. My research explores diffusion models, multimodal language models, reinforcement learning, and Vision-Language-Action models. Ultimately, I hope to make 3D generation useful for both creative applications and embodied AI, from producing visually appealing 3D assets to creating simulation-ready content, while also bringing 3D knowledge into robot learning and developing more capable and efficient VLA models.

Since August 2024, I have been a Research Resident at VinAI Research and then Qualcomm AI Research . Under the supervision of Phong Nguyen and in collaboration with Anh Tran , I have worked on various topics in 3D. This research has resulted in two publications on 3D generation: PixGS and Next3DGen .

Apart from AI, I enjoy reading about a wide range of subjects, such as philosophy and economics, as well as watching films and playing video games in my free time.
